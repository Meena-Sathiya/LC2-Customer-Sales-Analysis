# Customer & Sales Performance Analysis | Power BI

### Customer Segmentation • Sales Performance • Geographic Insights • Business Intelligence

## Project Overview

This project demonstrates an end-to-end customer and sales analysis solution developed using Microsoft Power BI and Excel, based on analytical work in a wholesale distribution environment.

The objective was to transform customer and sales records into meaningful business insights, helping stakeholders understand customer activity, sales distribution, geographic opportunities and operational performance.

**Portfolio privacy note:** The dashboard images in this repository use fictional/sample data to demonstrate the analytical approach. They do not reproduce confidential customer records or original commercial figures.

## Business Objectives

- Evaluate active, inactive and lapsed customer segments.
- Examine sales performance across branches and product categories.
- Identify customer retention and re-engagement opportunities.
- Analyse geographic customer distribution and postcode clusters.
- Explore sales representative allocation and potential territory improvements.
- Identify customer data-quality issues that could affect reporting and marketing.

## Analytical Insights

**Customer Retention**

Segmenting customers by purchasing activity helps identify dormant accounts and potential opportunities for re-engagement.

**Sales and Product Performance**

Branch and product-category analysis helps highlight revenue concentration and opportunities to review the sales mix.

**Geographic Analysis**

Customer postcode analysis can support targeted marketing, delivery-area planning and sales territory reviews.

**Sales Representative Allocation**

Identifying customers without assigned representatives can help improve account ownership and follow-up planning.

**Data Quality**

Reviewing missing or inconsistent customer attributes can support cleaner reporting and more reliable customer segmentation.

## Business Recommendations

1. Prioritise lapsed customers for targeted re-engagement.
2. Review opportunities to grow underrepresented product categories.
3. Use geographic clusters to support field-sales and delivery planning.
4. Review unassigned customer accounts for improved sales coverage.
5. Improve customer master-data completeness and consistency.

These are analytical recommendations, not claims of implemented operational improvements or verified financial savings.

## Project Outcome

This project demonstrates practical experience combining operational business knowledge with data preparation, customer segmentation, commercial analysis and Power BI reporting.

It highlights how data analytics can support decision-making across sales, customer service, marketing and operations.
